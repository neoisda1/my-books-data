# My Books data

Books for the My Books section of the Kids Dua app. The app doesn't bundle
them: it downloads each one when a reader asks for it, through jsDelivr, from
a tag of this repository.

## Tafsir al-Mizan (English), AI translation

`almizan/` holds Tafsir al-Mizan by Allamah Sayyid Muhammad Husayn
Tabataba'i, in the English translation made by **Furqan Apps** with
artificial intelligence from the original Arabic. There is one gzipped JSON
file per surah, plus `almizan_000` for the introduction.

In Furqan Apps' own words, the translation "has been generated from the
original Arabic text (sourced from lib.rafed.net) using advanced Artificial
Intelligence technology", "there may be errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in
the text", and it "should be used with appropriate discretion and scholarly
verification when needed. It is not a substitute for studying under qualified
teachers or consulting traditional scholarly works."

- **Source:** https://github.com/app-furqan/quran-app-data (commit
  `b950ee50aa96`), file `data/tafsir_almizan_en.db.tar.xz`.
- **Copyright:** (c) 2025 Furqan Apps.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International
  (CC BY-ND 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/ . The full
  notice is in `LICENSE`.
- **Verse translations:** the verses in English are Ali Quli Qara'i's
  translation.

**What was done to the text.** It was divided into daily reading passages,
and its Markdown formatting was taken off. Nine stray lines that are not part
of the translation were removed: leftovers from the AI process, such as
"thoughtful" and "Standard cleaning and formatting applied. No commentary
added.", and one heading duplicated inside a sentence. No wording of the
translation was changed. Corrections to the translation itself belong with
Furqan Apps (usersupport@furqan.app).

These files are generated by `generate_reading_books.py` in the app's
repository. Don't edit them by hand. A changed book is published under a new
tag; tags are never moved.

## Tafsir al-Mizan and Tafsir Namoona (Urdu), AI translations

`almizan_ur/` holds Tafsir al-Mizan by Allamah Tabataba'i, and `namoona_ur/`
holds Tafsir Namoona by Ayatullah Naser Makarem Shirazi and others. Both are
in the Urdu translations made by **Furqan Apps** with artificial intelligence:
Al-Mizan from the Arabic, Namoona from the Persian. There is one gzipped JSON
file per surah, plus `_000` for each introduction.

In Furqan Apps' own words, each Urdu translation was made "using advanced
Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology" (جدید مصنوعی ذہانت (AI) ٹیکنالوجی).
Readers are asked to "verify important information against the original
sources and to consult scholars" (صارفین کو اہم معلومات کو اصل ذرائع سے تصدیق
کرنی چاہیے اور مستند تشریحات کے لیے اہل علم اور علماء سے مشورہ کرنا چاہیے).

- **Source:** https://github.com/app-furqan/quran-app-data, at the same
  commit as above. The files are `data/tafsir_almizan_ur.db.tar.xz` and
  `data/tafsir_nemouneh_fa_en_ur.tar.xz` (the Urdu column).
- **Copyright and licence:** (c) 2025 Furqan Apps, CC BY-ND 4.0, as above.
- **Verse translations:** the verses in Urdu are Allama Zeeshan Haider
  Jawadi's translation, word for word.

**What was done to the text.**
- It was divided into daily passages, and its Markdown formatting was taken
  off, including the code markers around quoted Arabic. The Arabic stays
  where it stood.
- Stray lines left by the AI process were removed: 21 in Al-Mizan and 1 in
  Namoona, such as "thought" and "architectural_thought".
- Four false starts were removed, where a section broke off mid-sentence and
  began again: 1 in Al-Mizan and 3 in Namoona.
- One diagram held as image data was left out (Al-Mizan).
- Namoona's footnotes (its references) were left out, along with the printed
  volumes' 20 tables of contents.

No wording of the translations was changed.
